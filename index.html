Progression and recovery
  • Pick a weight that leaves 1-2 reps in reserve. When you hit the top of the rep range on every set, add the smallest jump (about 2.5 kg).
  • Rest 2-3 min on heavy lifts, 60-90 s on accessory work. The timer starts on its own when you tick a set.
  • Every 5th week is a deload: same exercises, about 40% less volume and lighter loads.
  • Land jumps softly and quietly. Stop jump sets if your form gets sloppy.
  • Fuel: roughly 1.6-2 g protein per kg (about 90-110 g a day at 55 kg), carbs around training, and 8+ hours of sleep.
